The Story

Bell John K

Engineer by training. Rationalist by conviction. Writer by compulsion.

I am an engineer by training, a rationalist by conviction, and a writer by compulsion. Not in that order. Never cleanly in any order.

By profession I work with Southern Railway, Trivandrum Division — electrical systems, infrastructure, contracts, the relentless business of keeping things running on time. It has taught me one thing above everything else: that clarity of thinking is not a luxury. It is the work.

The Beginning

In college I wrote poems. Romantic, dramatic, thoroughly painkili poems — the kind you are grateful nobody can find years later. I searched for those old notebooks recently. They are gone. Some things are better left unfound.

After college came the years I wouldn’t trade — friends, travel, noise, life lived loudly and together. Then in 2008 I joined the railways. Almost overnight, my world changed. Solitude — which had once felt like something to escape — slowly became something I looked forward to. It became, unexpectedly, where I did my best thinking.

The Hunger

That is when I started reading seriously. Not as a habit — more like a hunger. Sapiens by Harari broke something open in me. I’m currently working through Sean Carroll’s The Biggest Ideas in the Universe. These books make the world feel both larger and more intimate at the same time.

I started keeping notebooks. Scattered, honest, sometimes half-finished. During the Covid lockdown I went back through years of those notebooks and decided to bring order to them. That process, aided by AI tools, gave me an idea. And that idea became a book — Habitually Positive.

The Name

Long before writing, I had a passion for photography — especially dance and dancers. When I started an Instagram account to share my photographs, I wanted a name that felt like light. I was also deep in a phase of Spanish music. So I put the two together. Magia Ligera. Light Magic.

I chose it on instinct. Only later did I realise how well it fits — a home for ideas that illuminate quietly and shift something.

What I Believe

I am a genuine admirer of Article 51A(h) of the Indian Constitution — the duty of every citizen to cultivate scientific temper, humanism, and the spirit of inquiry. I carry that as a personal creed, not a legal obligation.

Real wonder begins where false certainty ends.

I don’t write self-help dressed in certainty. I write honest, evidence-grounded thinking about habits, decisions, leadership, and how to actually live well. Not perfectly. Honestly.

A Few Other Things

I am from Kerala. I think in Malayalam and write in English. Curious about politics, sceptical of consensus, endlessly interested in why people do what they do — not as a philosopher, but as an engineer who refuses to stop asking questions.

Who I Am Writing For

Honestly — I’m still figuring that out. But I think I am writing for the person I was before the notebooks. The one surrounded by noise who hadn’t yet discovered what solitude and reading can do to a person.

If something here makes you stop and think — even for a moment — that is enough.

Currently: Aluva, Kerala. Reading Sean Carroll. Thinking about thinking.
